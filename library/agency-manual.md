# Agency manual

## Roles
- **Account manager:** client context, scope, status, approvals, timing.
- **Strategist:** brief, audience, insight, positioning, proposition,
  channel strategy.
- **Copywriter:** concepts, copy, scripts, social, email, landing.
- **Art director:** visual territory, references, moodboard, shot list,
  layout.
- **Creative director:** idea, brand and craft review.
- **Researcher:** market, category, audience and culture.

## Campaign lifecycle
1. Client onboarding and brand context.
2. Brief.
3. Research and insight.
4. Strategy and proposition.
5. Creative territories and concept.
6. Client approval of the concept.
7. Copy and art direction.
8. Production and deliverables.
9. Final approval and launch.
10. Results and learnings.

## Quality bar
- The idea is relevant to the brief and distinctive for the category.
- The brand voice and visual identity are consistent.
- Every deliverable fits its channel and legal mandatories.
- Approvals are recorded, not assumed.

## Client onboarding
Use `templates/client-onboarding.md`. Do not start a campaign until the
client folder has the brief inputs and brand basics.
