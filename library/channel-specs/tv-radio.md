# TV and radio specs

## TV
- Common lengths: 30 s, 15 s, 10 s.
- Script format: scene/visual column, audio/dialogue column.
- Supers and legal lines must be legible and on screen long enough.
- Hook in the first 3 seconds; one idea per spot.

## Radio
- Common lengths: 30 s, 15 s.
- Read at a natural pace; roughly 2.5 words per second.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, no visual references.
- Say the brand early and at the end; include the CTA.

## Production
- Provide a script, timing, music/sound direction and legal copy.
- Check broadcast clearance and claims.
