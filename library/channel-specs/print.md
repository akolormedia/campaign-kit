# Print specs

## Formats
- Magazine, newspaper, posters, direct mail.
- Verify trim, bleed (often 3-5 mm), safe area and resolution.

## Art
- 300 dpi at final size for images.
- CMYK with the printer's profile; check rich black and overprint.
- Fonts outlined or embedded per the supplier.

## Copy
- Headline, subhead, body, CTA and legal line.
- Keep legal text legible; do not set it below the supplier's minimum.

## Production
- Provide a proof and sign-off before print.
- Keep the approved PDF in `exports/`.
