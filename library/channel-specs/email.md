# Email specs

## Subject and preheader
- Subject: 35-50 characters; specific, no spammy capitalization.
- Preheader: 40-90 characters; completes the subject, does not repeat it.

## Body
- One primary CTA.
- Short paragraphs; scannable subheads.
- Plain-text version for accessibility.
- Alt text for images.

## Technical
- Width: 600 px typical.
- Compressed images; avoid heavy backgrounds.
- Test in dark mode and mobile.

## Legal
- Include unsubscribe and required legal lines.
- Do not use a purchased list without consent.
