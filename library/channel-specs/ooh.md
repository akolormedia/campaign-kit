# Out-of-home specs

## Formats
- Billboards, transit, street furniture, posters.
- Verify dimensions, bleed, safe areas and viewing distance with the
  media supplier.

## Copy
- 3-7 words is a good target.
- One idea, one CTA or brand line.
- Legible at speed and from a distance; high contrast.

## Art
- Simple composition; one focal point.
- Avoid small legal text; place mandatories where they can be read.
- Provide print-ready files with the supplier's specs.
