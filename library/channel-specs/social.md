# Social channel specs

Always verify current platform specs before final art.

## Instagram
- Feed: 1080 x 1350 (4:5) or 1080 x 1080 (1:1).
- Stories/Reels: 1080 x 1920 (9:16), safe areas for UI.
- Feed copy: first 125 characters before "more".
- Reels: hook in the first 1-2 seconds; captions on.

## TikTok
- 1080 x 1920 (9:16), 9-60 s typical.
- Native, not polished-ad feel; captions and sound matter.
- Hook in the first 1-3 seconds.

## LinkedIn
- Feed image: 1200 x 627 or 1080 x 1080.
- Copy: professional, specific, no clickbait.
- Document/video formats for longer stories.

## X
- Short copy; one idea per post; thread for context.
- Image 1600 x 900 or 1080 x 1080.

## Best practices
- Adapt the idea to the platform, do not just resize.
- Provide captions/subtitles for video.
- Check legal line legibility.
