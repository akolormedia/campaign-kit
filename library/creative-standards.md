# Creative standards

## Idea
- One clear idea, not a collage of tactics.
- Relevant to the brief and to the audience's tension.
- Distinctive in the category; if it could be any competitor, rework it.

## Craft
- Copy: concrete, economical, in the brand voice.
- Art direction: one visual territory, intentional references.
- Layout: hierarchy, legibility, safe areas.
- Production: the idea survives the format.

## Brand
- Respect the brand platform, voice and visual identity.
- Do not use a claim the client cannot substantiate.
- Flag missing legal text and approvals.

## Review
- Review in order: idea, brand, craft, channel, legal.
- Give prioritized notes; do not rewrite the work.
- Approve only with explicit confirmation.
