# Presentation

## Goal
Turn the campaign work into a clear story for the client, not a data dump.

## Structure
Write `campaigns/<campaign>/deliverables/deck.md`:
1. Context and challenge
2. Objective and audience
3. Insight
4. Proposition
5. The idea
6. Executions by channel
7. Deliverables and timeline
8. Next steps / approvals

## Rules
- One message per slide.
- Use the campaign's actual copy and approved numbers.
- Keep the deck short; details go in the appendix.
- Mark missing data with `[PENDIENTE]`.
- The editor previews Markdown and can print/export a PDF; there is no
  slide editor.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Slide 4 — La idea:** "El equipaje emocional".
> **Nota:** abrir con la imagen de la recepción, no con el racional.

## Do not
- Do not present unapproved work as final.
- Do not invent results or benchmarks.
