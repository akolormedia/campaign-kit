# Brand voice

## Goal
Make every piece sound like the same brand, across channels and authors.

## Before writing
Read `clients/<client>/brand/voice.md` and `brand/manual.md`. If the voice
guide is empty, ask for examples and build it with the user before
writing campaign copy.

## What to apply
- Tone: warm, direct, playful, premium, etc.
- Lexicon: words and expressions the brand uses and avoids.
- Grammar: person, contractions, sentence length, capitalization.
- Do/don't with examples.
- Channel adaptation without losing the brand's core voice.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Do:** "Descansa de verdad. El mar se encarga del resto."
> **Don't:** "¡La mejor experiencia premium e inolvidable del Caribe!"

## Do not
- Do not use generic superlatives the brand would never say.
- Do not invent claims, prices or legal text to make the copy sound
  better.
