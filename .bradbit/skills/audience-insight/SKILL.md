# Audience insight

## Goal
Find the human tension the campaign can dramatize, supported by
evidence — not a demographic description.

## Process
1. Define the question: what the audience currently believes, feels or
   does, and what the campaign needs to change.
2. Gather sources: interviews, reviews, social conversation, category
   data, cultural references.
3. Separate:
   - verified fact (with source),
   - reported behavior (with source, variable),
   - your interpretation.
4. Write the insight as a tension:
   "I want X, but Y gets in the way."
5. Save in `campaigns/<campaign>/research/` and cite sources.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Observación:** las parejas reservan escapadas cortas, pero sienten
> culpa por "no descansar suficiente".
> **Insight:** quiero desconectar, pero siento que hasta el descanso
> tiene que ser productivo.

## Do not
- Do not present a stereotype as an insight.
- Do not use one source to claim a cultural generalization.
