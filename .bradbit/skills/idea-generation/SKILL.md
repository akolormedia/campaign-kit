# Idea generation

## Goal
Move from strategy to 2-3 genuinely different creative territories, then
to one campaign idea.

## Process
1. Read the brief, strategy, brand manual and channel specs.
2. Generate 2-3 territories. For each:
   - name,
   - one-line idea,
   - insight it taps,
   - example executions across channels,
   - why it fits the brand and the brief.
3. Check that the territories are different in mechanism, not just tone.
4. Save each as a one-pager in `concepts/`.
5. Recommend one only after the user has seen the options; explain the
   trade-offs.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Territorio:** "El equipaje emocional".
> **Idea:** cada huésped deja en la recepción una preocupación escrita;
> el hotel la guarda hasta el check-out.

## Do not
- Do not present three versions of the same idea.
- Do not promise image generation; art direction is a separate brief.
- Do not skip the rationale.
