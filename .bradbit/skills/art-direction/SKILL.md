# Art direction

## Goal
Give a designer, photographer or production team a clear visual direction
they can execute.

## Before writing
Read `brand/visual-identity.md`, `brand/manual.md`, the campaign brief,
strategy and approved concept. Check `clients/<client>/assets/` for
approved logos, images and fonts.

## Deliverables
Write in `campaigns/<campaign>/art-direction/`:
- visual territory and principles,
- reference list (with source and why),
- moodboard brief,
- shot list (subject, framing, light, location, props),
- layout notes per channel,
- asset needs and rights.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Territorio:** luz natural de primera hora, grano suave, agua en
> movimiento, nada de saturación turística.
> **Shot list:** plano detalle de manos soltando el teléfono en la arena.

## Do not
- Do not claim an image was generated; the editor does not generate
  images.
- Do not use references without checking usage rights.
- Do not contradict the brand's visual identity.
