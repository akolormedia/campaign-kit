# Creative review

## Goal
Improve the work with honest, prioritized notes — without rewriting it.

## Review order
1. **Idea:** distinctive, relevant to the brief, worth talking about?
2. **Brand:** fits the platform, voice and visual identity?
3. **Craft:** writing, art direction, layout, production quality.
4. **Channel:** does it work in each required format?
5. **Legal:** mandatories, substantiation, approvals.

## Output
Write notes in `campaigns/<campaign>/notes/`:
- what works,
- what to review (prioritized 3-5 points),
- questions for the author,
- recommended next step.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Funciona:** la idea del equipaje emocional es simple y produce una
> imagen clara.
> **A revisar:** el copy explica demasiado; dejar que la imagen haga el
> trabajo.

## Do not
- Do not rewrite the copy or art direction in the notes.
- Do not mark anything approved; the account manager records approvals.
