# Deliverables

## Goal
Turn the approved idea into the exact pieces the client needs.

## Process
1. Read the approved brief, strategy, concept, copy and art direction.
2. List every deliverable with channel, format, dimensions, duration,
   copy limits, due date and owner.
3. Produce the copy/art files in `deliverables/<channel>/`.
4. Check the channel spec and brand mandatories.
5. Name versions clearly: `v1`, `v2`, `final`, `approved`.
6. Record missing legal text or assets with `[PENDIENTE]`.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Entregable:** story 9:16, 15 s, subtítulos quemados, CTA "Reserva
> tu pausa", legal al final.

## Do not
- Do not mark a deliverable final without approval.
- Do not mix versions or overwrite approved files.
