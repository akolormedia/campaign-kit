# Campaign brief

## Goal
Produce a brief that a strategist, copywriter and art director can work
from without guessing.

## Before writing
Read `clients/<client>/client.md`, `brand/manual.md`,
`brand/mandatories.md`, `history/campaigns.md` and the campaign folder.
Ask for anything missing. Do not invent objectives, budgets, timing or
KPIs.

## Structure
Use `campaigns/_template/brief.md`:
background, business objective, communication objective, audience,
proposition, reasons to believe, deliverables, budget/timing and KPIs.

## Quality bar
- One clear business objective and one communication objective.
- A defined primary audience, not "everyone".
- A single-minded proposition.
- Deliverables and mandatories explicit.
- Gaps marked `[PENDIENTE]` / `[TODO]`, not filled with assumptions.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Objetivo de negocio:** aumentar 15% las reservas de fin de semana.
> **Objetivo de comunicación:** que la audiencia vea el hotel como una
> pausa real, no como otro destino de fotos.

## Do not
- Do not jump to ideas or copy before the brief is approved.
- Do not rewrite the client's business objective to make it easier.
