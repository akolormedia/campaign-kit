# Copywriting

## Goal
Write copy that is clear, on-brand, on-strategy and ready for the channel.

## Before writing
Read the brief, strategy, approved concept, `brand/voice.md` and the
relevant `library/channel-specs/` file. Ask for missing mandatories or
product facts.

## Craft
- Lead with the idea, not a warm-up.
- One message per piece.
- Use concrete language; cut generic superlatives.
- Give options when the brief is open: 3 headlines, 2 body routes, etc.
- Respect character counts and format limits.
- Mark missing legal text, prices or claims with `[PENDIENTE]`.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Headline:** "Descansa de verdad. El mar se encarga del resto."
> **Social:** "Tres días sin señal. El mar no necesita actualizaciones."

## Do not
- Do not invent a product claim, price, testimonial or legal line.
- Do not copy the same line across channels without adapting it.
