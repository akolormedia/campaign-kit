# Creative strategy

## Goal
Give the creative team a sharp strategic platform, not a description of
the category.

## Structure
Write `campaigns/<campaign>/strategy.md`:
- audience and current belief,
- insight,
- positioning,
- single-minded proposition,
- messaging hierarchy (primary, supporting, proof points),
- role of each channel,
- KPIs.

## Quality bar
- The proposition is one sentence and fits the brief.
- The messaging hierarchy has one primary message, not five.
- Each channel has a job in the funnel or journey.
- Claims are supported by the client or flagged `[PENDIENTE]`.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Posicionamiento:** el hotel donde el descanso no se siente como una
> tarea más.
> **Propuesta:** "Descansa de verdad. El mar se encarga del resto."

## Do not
- Do not turn the strategy into a list of tactics.
- Do not borrow a competitor's positioning without a reason.
