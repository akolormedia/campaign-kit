# Channel specs

## Goal
Produce work that fits the channel instead of forcing one format
everywhere.

## Sources
Read the relevant file in `library/channel-specs/`:
`social.md`, `digital.md`, `email.md`, `tv-radio.md`, `ooh.md`,
`print.md`.

## Process
1. Identify the exact placement and format.
2. Check dimensions, duration, character limits, safe areas and file
   requirements.
3. Adapt the idea and copy; do not just resize.
4. Note versioning and production needs in `production/`.
5. If a spec may be outdated, say so and verify before final art.

## Example (Spanish)
> **Instagram feed:** 1080 x 1350, texto legal legible, CTA en los
> primeros 125 caracteres.
> **Email:** asunto de 35-45 caracteres, preheader que complete la idea.

## Do not
- Do not invent platform dimensions; verify.
- Do not use the same copy length for a billboard and a landing page.
